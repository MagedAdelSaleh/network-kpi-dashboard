Outlier Controls

How a change gets flagged

  • We compare this month's change with the market's own past changes for that KPI.
  • It's flagged when it's far bigger than usual and big enough to matter for that KPI.

What's compared

  • Monthly KPIs: this month vs the two months before.
  • Quarterly KPIs (Jun, Sep, Dec, Mar only): the last three reported quarters.
  • Empty or barely loaded months are skipped.

Labels

  • Short history: under 10 months of data (monthly) or under 10 quarters (quarterly). Read with care.
  • Repeated Flag: last month's change was also unusual.

Plan view

Annual plan by market and cluster, against the previous fiscal year's actuals and, optionally, another plan version (revision). Open a basis label in the table to see how that KPI is compared.

Actuals vs plan

Method
  • The plan year always equals the fiscal year of the actuals month.
  • Annual total (TB, Mmin): year-to-date actual vs the plan phased by the previous year's actual monthly profile. Flat 1/12 phasing is never used. Without a complete previous year, attainment is shown with no verdict (except in March, when the full year is known).
  • Per month (Minutes/Month, MB/Month): the plan file stores these as 12-month sums, so the plan is divided by 12 and compared with the average monthly actual.
  • March volume ("Monthly ... [NW]" traffic): the plan file holds the March month's volume, so the latest month is projected to March on its trend since last March and compared with it.
  • A plan more than 5× away from the comparable actual is marked Check plan (usually a value entered in other units) and gets no verdict.
  • Year-end count (sites, cells, users, counts): the year-end projection at the run-rate since last March is compared with the target; required vs achieved monthly run-rate shown.
  • Year-end %: latest actual vs target in percentage points.
  • Verdict: On plan within the KPI's annual materiality (registry matY, proposed default); otherwise Above or Below plan, coloured only where the KPI has a defined good direction.
  • Clusters use the same rules as the dashboard (sums, ratios from summed components, population-weighted coverage). NL is excluded everywhere.

How the ranking works
Each KPI has a quarter-on-quarter and a year-on-year materiality threshold in the registry. A row appears when either is exceeded; its score is the larger multiple of threshold. Rows are ordered by the log of that score (so one huge percentage cannot crowd out the rest), raised 1.5× when the move is unusual against the market's own earlier quarterly changes (needs at least 3 earlier quarters to judge), raised 1.2× when the direction is unfavourable, and cut to 0.4× when the change exceeds 300% (usually reporting starting, not performance). KPIs without a good/bad direction are never labelled either way. Size KPIs (population) are excluded. Relative changes on very small bases (under 30 for site counts) are ignored. Weights and thresholds are proposed defaults.
